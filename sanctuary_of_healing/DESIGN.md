## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an environment of restorative opulence for an elite holistic wellness hospital. The atmosphere marries clinical credibility with the hushed, deeply restorative serenity of a five-star bespoke retreat. 

The aesthetic style is **Organic Luxury & Editorial Glassmorphism**:
- Generous, meditative negative space creates a sense of slow luxury and mental clarity.
- Micro-deliberate details such as hairline metallic rules, soft ambient light glows, and frosted glass navigation panels mimic high-end architectural wellness sanctums.
- The visual tension between classical high-contrast serif headlines and crisp modern grotesque secondary copy positions the practice as both timelessly grounded in natural medicine and state-of-the-art in integrative diagnostic care.
- Interactions are silky and unhurried: soft transitions, featherlight hovers, and luminous state transformations that soothe rather than overstimulate.

## Colors

The palette draws inspiration from sacred natural minerals, luminous botanical extracts, and warm alabaster.

- **Base Canvas (`#FBF9F4`):** A warm ivory that replaces sterile clinical whites with an organic, gentle foundation that reduces ocular fatigue.
- **Metallic Gold Hierarchy:**
  - Primary Accent (`#B8963E`): Deep burnished gold, utilized for active anchors, high-contrast borders, key interactive highlights, and focal badges.
  - Radiant Gold (`#E5C062`): Mid-tone metallic used for icons, interactive state transitions, and shimmering gradients.
  - Pale Gold (`#F3DC96`): Luminescent highlight tone used in gradient fills, subtle aura blurs, and tinted selection states.
  - *Standard Gold Gradient:* Linear gradient 135deg from `#F3DC96` via `#E5C062` to `#B8963E`.
- **Contrast & Depth (`#1F1F1F`):** Deep volcanic charcoal anchor, reserved for authoritative section blocks, evening contemplation modes, high-prominence headers, and grounding structural footers.
- **Editorial Grey (`#767676`):** Balanced neutral tone engineered specifically for body reading against the ivory canvas, keeping optical contrast compliant without the clinical sharpness of black.
- **Subtle Surface Tints:** Translucent gold washes (`rgba(184, 150, 62, 0.04)`) and warm cream container elevations (`#F5F0E6`) create stratified content layers without harsh divisions.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy orchestrates a dialogue between classical literary elegance and contemporary functional precision:

- **Headlines (`Playfair Display`):** Carries the soul of the center. Use regular weights to preserve the delicate contrast of the serif strokes. In display scenarios, italicize key emotional terms (e.g., *Restoration*, *Vitality*, *Holistic*) to heighten luxury cues.
- **Body & Structural Text (`Inter`):** Kept airy with increased letter spacing and generous line heights to preserve calm readability. Body paragraphs are rendered at `300` and `400` weights in `#767676` to soften visual weight on `#FBF9F4`.
- **Labels & Metatags:** Set in full uppercase with extended tracking (`0.08em` to `0.15em`) at small scales to echo luxury apothecaries and high-end editorial captions.

## Layout & Spacing

A structured 12-column layout designed with generous horizontal and vertical breathing room gives content an open, non-rushed presence.

- **Breakpoints:**
  - Mobile: `< 768px` (4 columns, `margin`: 1.25rem, `gutter`: 1rem)
  - Tablet: `768px – 1024px` (8 columns, `margin-tablet`: 2.5rem, `gutter`: 1.5rem)
  - Desktop: `> 1024px` (12 columns, `margin-desktop`: 4rem, `gutter-desktop`: 2.5rem, max content container 1360px centered)
- **Spacing Rhythm:** Vertical spacing leans heavily towards large, comfortable steps (`space-xl` and above) between conceptual sections. Do not crowd clinical offerings; allow sanctuary-style isolation around imagery and treatment narratives.
- **Alignment:** Centralized alignment for emotional editorial statements and doctor philosophies; left-aligned for data-dense clinical profiles, appointment itineraries, and treatment directories.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is soft and translucent, evocative of filtered natural sunlight through sheer linen and crystalline water.

- **Glassmorphism Layer (Navbar & Overlays):**
  - Background: `rgba(251, 249, 244, 0.72)`
  - Backdrop Filter: `blur(16px) saturate(140%)`
  - Border: 1px hairline `rgba(229, 192, 98, 0.25)` on the bottom edge
- **Ambient Spa Shadows:**
  - Card & Floating Elements: `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(184, 150, 62, 0.07), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(31, 31, 31, 0.03)`
  - Elevated Modal / Drawer: `0 24px 64px -12px rgba(31, 31, 31, 0.12), 0 0 0 1px rgba(229, 192, 98, 0.2)`
- **Dividers & Structural Rules:**
  - Hairline gold dividers: 1px solid linear gradient from `transparent` via `#E5C062` to `transparent` (for center dividers) or solid `rgba(184, 150, 62, 0.2)` (for structural grid delimiters).

## Shapes

The form factor combines organic fluidity with architectural order. 

- **Primary UI Radius:** Base `roundedness: 2` (0.5rem / 8px) gives small controls and inputs a soft, human touch while remaining crisp.
- **Container Surfaces (`rounded-lg` / 1rem):** Used on treatment cards, doctor profile modules, and booking panes.
- **Special Architectural Shapes:**
  - Hero image masks and treatment spotlight visual frames employ classical Roman arch shapes (`border-radius: 999px 999px 0 0`).
  - Small pills (`rounded-full`) are reserved for category tags, healing modality badges, and icon buttons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Book Treatment / Consultation):**
  - Background: Metallic gradient (`linear-gradient(135deg, #F3DC96 0%, #E5C062 50%, #B8963E 100%)`) with text in deep charcoal (`#1F1F1F`), semibold tracking.
  - Hover: Subtle brightness lift (`filter: brightness(1.04)`) and extended gold glow shadow (`0 8px 24px rgba(184, 150, 62, 0.28)`).
  - Padding: 0.875rem 2rem; border-radius: 0.5rem.
- **Secondary (Explore Modality):**
  - Background: Transparent; 1px border in `#B8963E`; text in `#1F1F1F`.
  - Hover: Background fills with `rgba(243, 220, 150, 0.12)`.
- **Tertiary / Link:**
  - Text in `#B8963E` with an animated underline starting from center out.

### Chips & Modality Badges
- Background: Warm ivory container tint (`#F5F0E6`) with a delicate hairline border (`1px solid rgba(184, 150, 62, 0.3)`).
- Text: Label-sm in `#1F1F1F` uppercase.
- Selected State: Gradient gold fill with dark charcoal typography.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Background: `#FFFFFF` or `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.7)`.
- Border: 1px solid `rgba(118, 118, 118, 0.25)`.
- Focus State: Border transitions to `#B8963E` with a diffuse halo: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(229, 192, 98, 0.15)`. No harsh system outlines.
- Placeholder text in `#767676` with high line-height clarity.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Rounded border with `#B8963E` rim; checked state filled with gold gradient and crisp white/charcoal glyph.

### Cards (Integrative Programs & Specialists)
- Background: `#FFFFFF` with warm undertone, 1px border `rgba(184, 150, 62, 0.15)`.
- Elevation: Ambient light gold drop shadow.
- Inner elements separated by thin horizontal gold hairline gradients.
- Hover: Gentle vertical translation (`-4px`) and deepening of the warm diffuse shadow.

### Specialized Components
- **Integrative Diagnostic Timeline:** Vertical hairline gold track linking treatment milestones with soft gold glowing pulse nodes.
- **Apothecary Prescription Accordion:** Deep charcoal and warm ivory layered surfaces revealing naturopathic compositions, clinical evidence, and preparation methods.
- **Practitioner Credential Drawer:** Glassmorphic side overlay detailed with gold monogram accents and serif credentials.
